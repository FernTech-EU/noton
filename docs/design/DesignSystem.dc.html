NOTON · THEME “CARNET”

A quiet paper notebook, one vermilion accent

Warm paper surfaces, ink text, vermilion for selection and the one primary action. Focus is always ink blue and drawn as a ring, so it never looks like selection. Fonts: Instrument Sans (UI), Literata (notes), JetBrains Mono. All OFL.
